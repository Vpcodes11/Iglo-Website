Nival — An imagined expedition

An interactive ice shelter in an arctic landscape. The opening sequence scans the shelter before revealing the landscape. Press Escape to skip the introduction. Hover over a brick to lift it; surrounding bricks lift and turn with a gradually smaller response, while distant bricks stay in place. Move off the brick to let it settle back into the igloo. Scroll to change your viewpoint. Use Page Down and Page Up, or Home to return to the beginning. Press P to pause or resume cursor response and snowfall.
